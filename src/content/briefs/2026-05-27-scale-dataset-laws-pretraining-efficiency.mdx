## What happened

Recent scaling-law work is revisiting the relationship between model size, data volume, and compute. The headline is not that scale stopped working, but that naive token accumulation is a weaker strategy than it used to be.

## Why it matters

If dataset quality changes the compute-optimal frontier, labs can buy progress through curation, filtering, and targeted mixtures rather than only through larger clusters.

## Technical context

Classic scaling laws assumed broad regularities across model and dataset size. Newer work stresses that mixture quality, domain coverage, contamination, and repetition alter the shape of those curves.

## What to watch next

Watch whether pretraining reports disclose enough about data composition to make scaling claims comparable across labs.
