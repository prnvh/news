## What happened

Evaluation designers are paying more attention to tasks that require memory across steps, episodes, or tool calls. One-shot prompts remain useful, but they miss important failures in systems that need to preserve context.

## Why it matters

Real work is stateful. Agents must remember constraints, revise plans, track intermediate results, and recover from mistakes. Evaluations that reset context too often can overstate practical capability.

## Technical context

Stateful evals test the interaction between model reasoning, memory representation, tool use, and environment feedback. They are harder to standardize, but closer to deployment.

## What to watch next

Look for benchmark suites that report persistence, recovery, and long-horizon reliability separately from raw task success.
